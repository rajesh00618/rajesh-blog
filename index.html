Current Research
PAM

Persistent Activation Memory

An experimental AI compression paradigm focused on making large language models significantly more efficient through reusable activation behavior during inference.

The core idea: can intelligence inside large models be partially represented through recurring activation patterns — instead of relying entirely on repeated full parameter execution?

If scalable, the concept could fundamentally change how large AI systems are deployed at the edge.
